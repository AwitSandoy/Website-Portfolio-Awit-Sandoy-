Our Story

The word Sariwa means "Fresh." At Sariwa Project, we believe that staying hydrated should be a simple, stylish, and refreshing part of your day.

Our Mission

We are dedicated to bringing freshness and simplicity to everyday hydration by creating thoughtfully designed bottles and tumblers built for modern lifestyles.

Why We Do It

We believe in quality over quantity. Every product we craft is made for durability and convenience, helping you reduce single-use plastic while keeping your favorite drinks at the perfect temperature.

Freshness in every sip. Freshness in every step.
